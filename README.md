# DiveAtlas User Guide

[Open DiveAtlas](https://diveatlas.site/) to explore dive sites and marine conditions on an interactive world map.

## Using the map

1. Search for a dive site or place, then select a result to move the map.
2. Open **Layers** and choose a map view. Use its controls to select a month, depth, or prediction time when available.
3. Click or tap the map to inspect information for that location. Select a dive-site marker for site details.
4. Turn overlays on or off independently of the selected map view.
5. Use the top-bar controls to change language, appearance, length units, and temperature units. Use **Share** to copy a link to the current map view.

The map remembers your selected view and overlay settings in your browser. Choose **None** in Layers to clear the environmental view; overlays can remain visible.

## Map views

| View | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Terrain** | Broad patterns in estimated seafloor slope. |
| **Water TEMP** | Typical monthly water temperature by depth. Select a location for available readings and a depth profile. |
| **Water Clarity** | Typical monthly water transparency from satellite observations. |
| **Regional Currents** | Typical monthly regional flow direction and speed. Select a month and depth. |
| **Waves** | Typical monthly wave height and period. Select a location for details or compare months. |
| **Tide** | A time-specific EOT20 tide estimate, with a local curve and predicted highs and lows where available. |
| **Dive Experience** | A historical monthly outlook combining available physical and ecological evidence. Select a location for its score, confidence, and supporting information. |
| **Reef Condition** | Historical ocean heat and reef thermal-stress indicators. |

## Overlays

Overlays add map details independently of the selected view: **Terrain**, **Depth contours**, **Reef extent**, **Coral records**, **Fish density**, and **Dive sites**. Availability varies by location and zoom level.

## Understanding results

Marine coverage, time periods, and map resolution vary by data source. An unavailable reading or an unshaded area means the map has no supported result there; it does not prove that a feature is absent.

Water temperature, clarity, currents, and waves describe typical historical conditions, not live local measurements. Tide results are model estimates and may not capture reefs, narrow channels, harbors, or other local effects. Dive Experience is an outlook, not a forecast or dive-safety rating. Reef thermal-stress indicators describe environmental pressure; they do not confirm bleaching or mortality.

DiveAtlas is for exploration, not navigation or safety decisions. Check current local conditions and use appropriate navigation equipment before diving.

## Sources

DiveAtlas combines data from OBIS, UNEP-WCMC, AODN/NRMN, GEBCO, NOAA, NOAA Coral Reef Watch, EOT20, and Copernicus Marine. Select the information icon in a layer or result for details where available. Browse [dive sites](https://diveatlas.site/dive-sites/) or [regions](https://diveatlas.site/regions/).
