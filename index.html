DiveAtlas

 Coral reef distribution

DiveAtlas is an interactive global map for scuba divers. Explore mapped dive sites alongside coral records, reef extent, bathymetry and depth contours. These layers bring dive locations and marine geographic data together for browsing; they describe mapped records and seafloor context, not current dive conditions.
